The RSAK tenets

Seven words we live by

Students say these together in class. Over time they stop being words on the studio wall and start being who you are.

  1. Honesty

    Tell the truth, even when it costs you something.

  2. Courtesy

    Treat everyone with respect, on the mat and off it.

  3. Modesty

    Let your actions speak. Stay humble as you grow.

  4. Integrity

    Know what is right, and do it when no one is watching.

  5. Loyalty

    Stand by your family, your friends, and your school.

  6. Perseverance

    Keep showing up. Finish what you start.

  7. Indomitable spirit

    Face hard things with courage and a steady heart.

The path to black belt

We believe what you become matters far more than the belt you earn. Still, every belt marks real growth, and students move up through Progress Checks, also called stripe testing, so there is always a next goal in sight.

  1. STEP 1

    White Belt

    Your first belt. You earn it by finishing your introductory lessons with Mr. L.

  2. STEP 2

    Basic Training

    Core kicks, strikes, blocks, stances, and your first forms.

  3. STEP 3

    Black Belt Training

    Intermediate and advanced training. A black stripe through the center of the belt marks a student on the road to leadership.

  4. STEP 4

    Junior Ranks

    Advanced ranks for younger students preparing for full black belt.

  5. STEP 5

    Black Belt

    The goal was never just the belt. It is the character built along the way, and the start of a life lived with black belt excellence.

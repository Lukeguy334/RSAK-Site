Every belt is earned on the inside first

We believe what you become matters far more than the belt you earn. Each step toward black belt asks a student to grow in character, and these four marks are what we look for.

Self-control

Power means little without restraint. Students learn to be quick to listen, slow to anger, and in charge of their own actions.

Perseverance

Nobody gets it right the first time. We teach students to keep showing up, keep working, and finish what they start.

"Let us run with patience the race that is set before us." Hebrews 12:1

Indomitable spirit

Courage to face hard things with a steady heart. On the mat, at school, and at home, our students learn they are stronger than they think.

Black belt excellence

A black belt is a way of living. Honesty, respect, and humility, carried with integrity even when no one is watching.

A school built on character

Mr. Silva opened RSAK in 1990 on a simple idea: every child and adult deserves the best instruction possible to become a black belt in life.

Our hearts are always for our students, and they come first before anything else in our studio. Our goal is a place that is both nurturing and exciting, where students discover the best version of themselves, physically and mentally.

Meet your instructors

Our full-time instructors teach because martial arts shaped their own lives, and they want to pass that on.

Mr. Silva

Founder and Head Instructor, 6th Degree Black Belt

David "Rocky" Silva started training at age 12 and earned his 1st Degree Black Belt in 1984. He competed in South Africa in 1987 and 1989 as one of the top point fighters in the nation.

He opened Rocky Silva's American Karate in 1990, driven by his love for the martial arts and for helping others grow through training. He was inducted into the Martial Arts Hall of Fame in 1999 and received his 6th Degree Black Belt in 2012.

Off the mat

Spending time with his sons Maxwell and Lucas, running, riding motorcycles, and cheering on the New England Patriots.

Mr. Lazouras

Program Director, 4th Degree Black Belt

Andrew Lazouras, known to students as Mr. L, has trained at RSAK since 1995. He holds a 4th Degree Black Belt in American Tae Kwon Do and a 1st Degree Black Belt in Hapkido, and teaches in every program at the studio.

He is also the founder and CEO of Kicking for Causes, a nonprofit that has raised more than $130,000 for people in need. Mr. L holds graduate degrees in Sociology and Management and an MBA from Bryant University. New students start with him during their introductory lessons on the way to earning a white belt.

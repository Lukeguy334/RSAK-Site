Mr. Silva's story

Everything he teaches, he learned the hard way.

David "Rocky" Silva grew up in a house with seven brothers and sisters. When he started martial arts at age 12, he paid for his lessons by staying after class to clean the floor mats.

That work ethic never left him. He earned his 1st Degree Black Belt in 1984, then competed in South Africa in 1987 and 1989 as one of the top point fighters in the nation.

In 1990 he opened Rocky Silva's American Karate, driven by his love for the martial arts and for helping others grow through training. He has since been inducted into the Martial Arts Hall of Fame and the Warren Athletic Hall of Fame, and earned his 6th Degree Black Belt.

Ask around town and you will hear the same things about him: strong character, always ready to help, and a friendly, caring way that makes everyone who walks through the door feel welcome.

Our mission

Mr. Silva opened RSAK in 1990 on a simple idea: every child and adult deserves the best instruction possible to live their life with black belt excellence.

Our hearts are always for our students, and they come first before anything else in our studio. Our goal is a place that is both nurturing and exciting, where students discover the best version of themselves, physically and mentally.

We measure success not by the color of your belt, but by your character.

We instill black belt principles like discipline, focus, and indomitable spirit, and we see it as our duty to develop the leader in every student through example, encouragement, and a positive attitude.

Mr. Lazouras

Program Director, 4th Degree Black Belt

Andrew Lazouras, known to students as Mr. L, has trained at RSAK since 1995. He holds a 4th Degree Black Belt in American Tae Kwon Do and a 1st Degree Black Belt in Hapkido, and teaches in every program at the studio.

He is also the founder and CEO of Kicking for Causes, a nonprofit that has raised more than $130,000 for people in need. Mr. L holds graduate degrees in Sociology and Management and an MBA from Bryant University. New students start with him during their introductory lessons on the way to earning a white belt.

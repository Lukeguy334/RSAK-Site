Giving back

Dedicated to charity and service

At RSAK, we teach children and adults to believe in something bigger than themselves.

"It is every man's obligation to put back into the world at least the equivalent of what he takes out of it."
Albert Einstein

Many students think first about developing their body and mind, but what about their spirit? We believe the key to real growth is building the heart of a black belt in every student and family. Along with the principle and value-based talks we share in class, we give back to the world around us, and we partner with organizations that share the values we teach.

$130K+raised for those in need

Kicking for Causes

Each year, RSAK students and families set out to raise money and awareness for important causes. Kicking for Causes gets young people and the community involved in hands-on giving across the region. Break a Kicking for Causes karate board and you help several nonprofit organizations at once.

Founded by Program Director Mr. Lazouras, the program also runs self-defense clinics that build confidence for members of our community. Past partners include:

  • American Heart & Stroke Association
  • Alzheimer's Association
  • American Cancer Society
  • Camp Surefire

Want help fundraising for your cause? Email KickingCauses@gmail.com.

6,200children helped in one year

Helping the less fortunate

Every holiday season, RSAK works with neighboring faith communities to raise funds and collect items for those in need. Our friends at His Providence Church have welcomed us into their Bags of Hope campaign, which helps children in nearby adoption institutions.

In one recent year, we helped raise several thousand dollars toward goods and necessities for nearly 6,200 children across Rhode Island and Massachusetts. We also collect items and donate each year to the Providence Rescue Mission, which supports families in need in our area.

Equal opportunity and education

As each school year begins, RSAK partners with local public and private schools to offer students valuable extracurricular programs, while helping PTO and PTA groups raise funds to keep those programs going.

We also volunteer to run seminars and classroom lessons, each tailored to what teachers want to improve. If you are part of a local PTO or PTA, or a teacher in the community, we can organize it with little to no work on your part.

Gift baskets that pack a punch

Over the past decade, we have donated hundreds of gift baskets to local schools, fundraisers, camps, workplace events, auctions, and raffles. Each one usually includes a trial program, student novelties, and a certificate for a free month of lessons or a private training seminar.

To request a basket, email info@rockysilva.com with a flyer or event details, or call 508-336-6669.
